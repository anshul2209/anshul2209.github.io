Work

Platform work at OLX — selected threads from recent years.

Edge caching and bot classification

Traffic classification and differential caching across page types so humans, search crawlers, and other bots hit the edge strategy that fits them. CDN cache hit moved from ~30% to ~55–60%; about 50K abusive bot and parasite-SEO requests are blocked daily, protecting crawl quality and origin load.

SEO at scale

Technical and on-page SEO work over 14 months drove about +160% daily organic clicks and +50% impressions, with a 99.5% reduction in 404s via expired-listing redirects and zero-inventory suppression — written up in two parts on tech.olx.in.

AI discoverability

Making the site legible to LLM crawlers: robots.txt Content-Signal directives, an llms.txt, and markdown content negotiation so agents can pull structured answers without scraping the full PWA shell.

Googlebot event-loop incident, Apr 2026

After a core update, deep filter and tracking-param URLs saturated the Node event loop. HPA was scaling on the wrong signal; vertical CPU scaling restored headroom while the URL surface was tightened.

Trunk-based development with session flags

Session-scoped flags in the SSR pipeline let the team merge small changes continuously without exposing unfinished work. Merge time halved and PR throughput rose about 4× — written up on tech.olx.in.
